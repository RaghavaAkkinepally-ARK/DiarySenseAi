# DiarySenseAi
# 🐄 DairySense AI

### AI-Powered Livestock Decision Support System

> “From data to decisions — AI-powered livestock intelligence.”

---

## 📌 Overview

DairySense AI is an AI-powered livestock decision-support system designed to improve dairy productivity through intelligent milk yield prediction and early health risk detection.

The system combines:

* Machine Learning
* Veterinary-informed biological logic
* Predictive analytics

to help transform traditional dairy management into a data-driven intelligent ecosystem.

This project was developed as:

* 🎓 CFAI Academic Project
* 🧬 BioAI Prototype Concept
* 🚀 Startup-Oriented AI Platform

---

# 🎯 Problem Statement

Dairy farmers often face:

* Unpredictable milk production
* Late disease detection
* Lack of intelligent monitoring systems
* Reduced productivity due to delayed intervention

Most existing systems mainly focus on:

* Data storage
* Animal registration
* Record maintenance

but lack:

* Predictive intelligence
* Early warning systems
* Actionable recommendations

---

# 💡 Proposed Solution

DairySense AI introduces a predictive intelligence layer for dairy management.

The system analyzes:

* Breed
* Age
* Lactation stage
* Feed quality
* Water intake
* Temperature
* Appetite & activity

and predicts:

* 🥛 Milk Yield
* 🩺 Health Risk
* 💡 Recommendations

# 📊 Results

### 📈 Performance Metrics

* Milk Prediction Error: ±1–2 Litres
* Health Prediction Accuracy: ~80–90%

### 📌 Key Insights

* Water intake strongly influences milk production
* Heat stress reduces yield
* Appetite/activity help identify health risks
